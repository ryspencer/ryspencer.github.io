Emma privacy policy

Emma is a personal tool used only by its owner, Ryan Spencer, on his own Gmail account.

  • Emma reads, labels, archives and moves messages to Trash in the owner's Gmail account, and sends emails to the owner. It cannot permanently delete email.
  • To decide how to sort a message, Emma sends that message's content to Anthropic's Claude API.
  • Emma keeps a log of its actions in the owner's private GitHub repository.
  • No data is sold, shared with advertisers, or used for any purpose other than organizing the owner's own inbox.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
